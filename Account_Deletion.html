What Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, the personal information and account data associated with your Tax Doctors account will be deleted, subject to any data that we are legally required to retain.

This may include:

  • Account information
  • Name and contact information
  • Login/authentication information
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where required by law, regulatory requirements, fraud prevention, security purposes, accounting requirements, or other legitimate legal obligations. Any retained information will only be kept for the period required for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

We will review and process account deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving and verifying the request.
