Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Tax Doctors ("we", "us", "our", or "Tax Doctors") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the Tax Doctors mobile application ("App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our services.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password information in securely protected/hashed form
  • Account and profile information

Financial and Transaction Information

  • Income and expense records
  • Transaction amounts and categories
  • Vendor or merchant names
  • Transaction dates and notes
  • Account balances
  • Receipt information

Tax and Service Information

  • CNIC information
  • Tax year and income sources
  • Tax-related and business information
  • Information submitted with service requests
  • Documents required for tax or other services

2. App Permissions

The App requests certain permissions only when they are required for specific features.

Camera Permission

Camera access is used when you choose to photograph bills, receipts, CNIC documents, or other documents required for our services. The camera is not accessed continuously or in the background.

Photos / Gallery Permission

Gallery access allows you to select bills, receipts, CNIC documents, salary certificates, bank statements, and other documents that you choose to upload through the App.

Notifications Permission

Notifications may be used for service-request updates, account-related information, important App notifications, and messages or updates from Tax Doctors. You can disable notifications through your device settings.

Phone / Calling Functionality

The App allows you to contact Tax Doctors support by opening your device's phone dialer. The App does not record phone calls, monitor calls, or access your call history. A phone call is initiated only when you choose to use the call-support feature.

3. Receipt Scanning and OCR

The App provides a bill and receipt scanning feature. When you choose to scan a receipt, the selected image may be processed using an optical character recognition (OCR) service to extract information such as the amount, vendor/merchant name, and date.

The OCR functionality may involve transmitting the selected receipt image to our third-party OCR service provider, OCR.space, for processing. We do not use receipt images for advertising or unrelated purposes.
